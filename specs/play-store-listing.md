## Full Description (max 4000 characters)

```
Storia brings beautifully illustrated children's storybooks to life with professional narration and ambient soundscapes.

Browse a curated library of stories, each featuring hand-crafted illustrations, page-by-page voice narration, and immersive background audio that matches each scene. Whether your child is reading along independently or listening at bedtime, Storia makes story time magical.

FEATURES

• Illustrated Storybooks — Browse a growing library of beautifully illustrated stories for children ages 5–12.

• Professional Narration — Every page is narrated by voice actors, with word-by-word highlighting so early readers can follow along.

• Ambient Soundscapes — Each scene is paired with gentle background audio that brings the story world to life.

• Audio Controls — Adjust narration and soundscape volume independently, or toggle either on and off to suit your child's preference.

• Simple & Safe — No ads, no in-app purchases aimed at children, and no account required. Just open and start reading.

• Offline-Friendly — Book covers and images are cached for a smooth experience, even on slower connections.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Open Storia and browse the story library.
2. Tap a book cover to start reading.
3. Swipe through illustrated pages at your own pace.
4. Tap the audio controls to adjust narration and soundscape settings.

DESIGNED FOR FAMILIES

Storia is built with families in mind. The app contains no advertising, no social features, and no external links accessible to children. Content is curated and reviewed before publishing. We encourage parents and guardians to enjoy stories together during family reading time.

For our privacy practices, visit: storia.kids/privacy-policy
```
